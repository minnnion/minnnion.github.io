    Pairing a phone, step by step

    Code 482 913 · expires in 5 minutes

    1. Open Phones on the PC. A QR code shows while that page is open. It holds the PC's address on your network, the port (47800 unless you change it), a one-time 6-digit code, and a fingerprint of the PC's certificate.
    2. Scan it with the phone. Point the phone's camera at the code and open the link. The phone connects and checks the PC's certificate matches the fingerprint, so it can't be fooled by another computer.
    3. The code is checked. It lasts 5 minutes and stops working after 5 wrong tries. You can turn on a matching number to compare on both screens.
    4. The phone gets its own key. The PC keeps only a scrambled copy of it, so even the saved file can't be used to pose as your phone. Remove a phone and its key stops working at once.
    5. From then on it reconnects by itself whenever Minniondeck is running and the phone is on the same network, and gets your latest buttons.
    Your first button

    From empty key to working button

    +

    1. Click an empty key

    On the Deck, click any empty spot. The Edit button window opens.

    2. Pick what it does

    Press keys, type text, open a program or website, change the volume, mute the mic, wait, turn the page, or a plugin's action. Add several steps for a macro.

    3. Make it look right

    A colour, an icon or app logo, a picture or GIF, a press animation, and states that change its look, like Mic on and Mic muted.

    4. Save it

    It's on the PC and on every paired phone straight away. Click it on the PC to test it.

    Everything inside

    What Minniondeck has

    Deck

    • Pages and folders, with a Back button inside folders
    • 3 × 3, 4 × 3 or 5 × 3 grids
    • Square buttons: picture in the middle, name at the bottom
    • Move buttons in Edit layout, nudge them with the arrow keys

    Buttons

    • 10 built-in actions, plus plugin actions
    • Several steps on one button, like a macro
    • Up to 8 looks on one button
    • 15 press effects, GIFs and live widgets

    Looks

    • 3400+ app and game logos, 1000+ pixel icons
    • Button designs made of layers
    • Your own pictures and GIFs with Import
    • Light and dark themes, three animation levels

    Profiles

    • A set of pages for each use
    • Switch by itself when an app comes to the front
    • Open where you left off, or on a page you pick
    • Share as .mdprofile files

    Phones

    • Pair with a QR code, as many phones as you like
    • Phones can follow the page open on the PC
    • Keep the screen awake, vibrate on press
    • The Phones page shows which need an update

    Plugins

    • Install from the marketplace in one click
    • Each runs apart, so a crash never takes the app down
    • Signed plugins, Developer mode for your own
    • Their own settings, kept across updates

    Safe and steady

    • Saves by itself, a whole file at a time
    • Up to 5 backups, and one before every import
    • Export and import all your profiles
    • A log for tracking down problems

    On your PC

    • Start with Windows, in the tray
    • Keep running when you close the window
    • Nothing goes to the cloud, no account
    • Updates itself from GitHub
