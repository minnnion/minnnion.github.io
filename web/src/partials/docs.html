    Welcome to Minniondeck

    Minniondeck turns your phone into a deck of buttons for your PC. These pages explain how it works, how to make buttons, pictures and plugins, and where everything is kept.

    How it fits together

    Phone→ Wi-Fi → Minniondeck on your PC→ Apps and plugins Your phone shows the buttons. The PC does the work. Nothing goes through the internet.

    You press a key on the phone. The phone tells Minniondeck on your PC, and Minniondeck runs what the button does: it presses keys, opens a program, changes the volume, or asks a plugin to talk to another app. Live buttons, like your CPU use or what Spotify is playing, update on the phone as things change.

    You can also click any button in the Deck on the PC. That's handy for testing a button before you reach for the phone.

    Getting started

    Install and pair your phone

    1. Install on your PC

    1. Download the installer from the Download page. It needs Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
    2. Run it.
    3. Pick what you will use it for. Setup downloads the plugins that go with it. You can add more later from the marketplace.

    2. Pair your phone

    There's no phone app to install. Your deck opens in the phone's web browser.

    1. Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi as the PC.
    2. On the PC, open Phones in the left rail. A pairing QR code shows while that page is open.
    3. Scan the code with the phone's camera and open the link. It works on Android and iPhone.
    4. Press Allow on the PC. Your deck opens in the phone's browser. Add it to the home screen and it opens full screen, like an app.
    5. If Show a matching number when a new phone pairs is on (Settings › Phones), check that both screens show the same number.

    The pairing code only works while the Phones page is open. Leave the page and it stops, so nobody else can pair later.

    Using the app

    The Deck, pages and folders

    The Deck is where your buttons live. It looks the same on the PC and on your phone.

    The left rail

    The rail shows one icon per page. Point at an icon to see its name.

    PageWhat it's for
    DeckYour pages and buttons. Make and edit buttons here.
    ProfilesSets of pages, and switching between them.
    PhonesPair phones and see which are connected.
    MarketplaceBrowse plugins and install them, read live from GitHub.
    SettingsStart-up, look, phones, plugins, backups and updates.

    Pages and folders

    • A profile holds pages. Pages can sit inside folders, and a folder opens its first page.
    • Use + Page and + Folder in the pages panel. Double-click a name, or press F2, to rename it.
    • The arrow at the left of the page bar, or Ctrl+B, closes the pages panel to give the buttons more room. Do it again to open it.
    • With Show a Back button inside folders on (Settings › Profiles and pages), pages in a folder get a Back button in their first free spot.
    • A page is a grid: 3 × 3, 4 × 3 or 5 × 3 keys. The phone shows it on its side.

    Buttons

    Every button is one square key: its picture in the middle and its name small at the bottom. Click an empty key (+) to make one, double-click a button to edit it, and right-click it for everything else.

    Settings › Appearance changes how big buttons are drawn on the PC and whether their names show. Hiding names hides them on phones too.

    Using the app

    Make a button

    1. On the Deck, click an empty key (+). The New button window opens.
    2. Go through its four steps: a name, what it does, a picture, and a colour. The preview shows it live.
    3. Press Add button. The button is on the PC and on your phone at once.

    The Edit button window

    This is where you make a button and change it later. To open it for a button you already have, double-click the button, or right-click it and pick Edit button. The preview is on the left, the four steps on the right.

    PartWhat you can do
    PreviewThe button as it looks on the deck and on your phone. Try it plays its press effect.
    1. What is it called?The button's name, shown small at the bottom of the key.
    2. What does it do?Search every action, or filter by Built in or by a plugin, then fill in its boxes. The actions are in the table below.
    3. Picture35 simple pictures, 1,036 pixel icons and 3,466 app logos, with search. Make your own pictures on this website, then Import them.
    LooksUp to 8 looks. Each press shows the next one, then back to the first. Each look has its own name, text, picture and colour. Use + Add look in step 3.
    4. ColourThe button's colour and its press effect: how it moves when you press it.
    Add button, CancelAdd button (or Save on a button you already have) keeps it. Cancel, ✕ or Esc leave everything as it was.

    If a step is missing, like an empty name, the bar at the bottom says which one before it adds the button.

    Other things you can do with a button

    Right-click a button for its menu:

    • Edit button opens the window above.
    • Duplicate makes a copy on the same page.
    • Copy and Paste put a copy on any page.
    • Move to page sends it to another page.
    • Delete removes it.

    To test a button, click it on the Deck: it runs, just like a press on your phone.

    Several steps on one button

    A button can run steps one after another, like a macro: open a website, wait 3 seconds, type some text. Add steps in the Edit button window and put a Wait between them where an app needs time.

    Looks

    Some buttons have more than one look, such as Mic on and Mic muted, or a plugin's Off, Recording and Paused. Each look can have its own picture, colour and name, so the key shows what's going on. Small dots in its corner show which look is on.

    Using the app

    Profiles and auto-switching

    A profile is a set of pages and folders: one for streaming, one for games, one for everyday use.

    Switch automatically

    1. Turn on Switch profiles automatically in Settings › Profiles and pages.
    2. On the Profiles page, choose the app for a profile, for example OBS Studio for Streaming.
    3. When that app comes to the front, Minniondeck switches to the profile. Using Minniondeck itself never switches.

    Open on start

    Settings › Profiles and pages › Open on start picks where Minniondeck opens: where you left off, or a page you choose.

    Share a profile

    • Export a profile on the Profiles page to get a .mdprofile file. It's sealed, so it can only be read by Minniondeck.
    • Double-click a .mdprofile file, or use Import, to add it. A backup is made first.
    • If the profile uses plugins you don't have, Minniondeck lists them as not installed.
    Using the app

    Make images and GIFs

    Pictures and GIFs for a button are made in the editor that comes with MinnionDeck. It opens in your browser and syncs straight back to the button.

    Edit a button's picture

    1. In MinnionDeck, open the button's Edit window and press Edit image.
    2. The editor opens in your browser with that button's picture: Images for a picture, GIF maker for a GIF.
    3. Change it, then press Sync to MinnionDeck. The Edit window shows it at once; press Save there to keep it.

    Press Edit image on another button while the editor is open, and the same browser tab opens that button.

    You can also bring in a file you made elsewhere: in the Edit window, Image or GIF, then Import….

    Button designer

    • Pick the button size first: 1 × 1, 2 × 1, 1 × 2 or 2 × 2. The download fits that size exactly.
    • Background: one colour, two colours, a gradient or a picture, with rounded corners if you like.
    • Parts on top: text, icons, shapes (box, circle, badge) and your own pictures, each with an outline and a drop shadow. Click a part to pick it, then drag it or use the arrow keys.
    • Parts are drawn in list order; ↑ and ↓ move one in front or behind.

    Where they're kept

    • In the editor: work you haven't synced is kept in your browser, under Saved in this browser.
    • In Minniondeck: imported files go in library\media. See Where your files are.
    • GIFs and designs made in older versions of the app keep working on their buttons, and Adjust still changes a design's colours, sizes and places.
    Using the app

    Where your files are

    Everything Minniondeck saves is on your own PC, in one folder:

    %APPDATA%\com.minnion.deck\

    Settings › About shows the exact path, with a button to open the logs folder.

    In that folderWhat's in it
    library\library.jsonProfiles, folders, pages and buttons
    library\media\Pictures and GIF files you imported, one file each
    settings.jsonEverything on the Settings page
    devices.jsonPaired phones
    backups\Up to 5 encrypted backups of your library
    plugins\<id>\Each installed plugin's own folder
    plugin-data\<id>\Each plugin's settings, kept apart so they survive updates
    logs\minniondeck.logWhat the app did, for fixing problems. The older log is minniondeck.old.log
    tiles\Street map tiles the live map has loaded, kept for 14 days

    Don't edit these files while Minniondeck is running. It writes them safely, a whole file at a time, and may write over your change.

    Backups

    • Back up when Minniondeck starts (Settings › Backup) makes one only when something changed.
    • A backup is also made before every import and every restore.
    • Keep 1, 3 or 5. Restore one from Settings › Backup; Minniondeck restarts with it.
    • Export all profiles… saves every profile as files you can copy to another PC.
    Plugins

    How plugins work

    A plugin adds actions, icons, GIFs and settings to Minniondeck: Spotify, Timer, Soundboard and Weather are all plugins.

    What a plugin is

    • A folder in plugins\<id>\ with manifest.json, actions.json, a DLL, and icons\ and gifs\.
    • It's shared as one .mdplugin file, which is that folder zipped.

    How they run

    • Plugins run in a helper copy of Minniondeck, not in the app itself. If a plugin crashes, the helper restarts and your app keeps going.
    • After 3 crashes in a minute, plugins stay stopped until you press reload in Settings › Plugins.
    • When a press fails, the error shows on the button.

    Signed plugins and Developer mode

    • A plugin runs straight away when it's signed by an author your PC trusts. Official plugins are signed by Minniondeck.
    • A damaged or changed plugin is refused.
    • An unsigned plugin installs, but only runs with Developer mode on (Settings › Plugins).
    • To trust another author, add their .mdkey under Trusted authors.

    Plugins run with the same rights as Minniondeck. Install only plugins from people you trust, and turn Developer mode on only for plugins you know.

    Plugin settings

    A plugin with settings has a Settings button in Settings › Plugins, for things like your Spotify Client ID. Passwords are encrypted by Windows and never shown again.

    Plugins

    Install and update plugins

    From the marketplace

    1. On the marketplace, press Install on a plugin.
    2. Your browser asks to open Minniondeck. Allow it.
    3. Minniondeck shows the plugin, downloads it from GitHub and checks it matches the marketplace's checksum.
    4. It shows who signed it. Press Install. The plugin starts at once, no restart.

    From a file

    Double-click a .mdplugin file, or open Settings › Plugins and choose Install a plugin…. Minniondeck shows what it is and asks before installing.

    Updating a plugin

    • Install the newer version the same way. It replaces the old one, and the popup says which version you have now.
    • Your plugin settings are kept, because they live in plugin-data\, apart from the plugin.
    • Need an older version back? Each plugin's page on the marketplace lists every version with its own Install button.

    Minniondeck checks your plugins for updates once a day (Settings › Plugins). A plugin with a newer version gets an Update button and a badge on the Marketplace page.

    Turn off or remove

    In Settings › Plugins, Turn off stops a plugin without removing it. Remove deletes it. Buttons that use it stay on your pages but won't run until it's back.

    Plugins

    Make a plugin

    Plugins are written in Rust with the plugin kit. The template builds, packs and installs itself, so you can see your first action on the deck in a few minutes.

    1. Start a project

    In a terminal, from the folder Minniondeck is installed in:

    Minniondeck.exe plugin new my_plugin --dir C:\code

    The id uses lower-case letters, digits and _.

    2. Build and try it

    Double-click build.cmd in the new folder. It:

    1. installs Rust the first time, if this PC doesn't have it (about 300 MB, once),
    2. builds the plugin,
    3. packs it as dist\my_plugin-<version>.mdplugin,
    4. installs it into Minniondeck, which loads it straight away.

    Then open a button's Edit window: What it does › Action › My plugin. build.ps1 -NoInstall only builds and packs.

    3. The files

    FileWhat it is
    src\lib.rsWhat each action does when pressed
    actions.jsonYour actions: id, label, group, icon, fields, states
    manifest.jsonId, name, version, DLL, icons, GIFs and settings. Change version for each release
    icons\PNG, GIF, SVG, JPG or WEBP, up to 512 KB each
    gifs\GIFs that come with the plugin, up to 2 MB each
    kit\The plugin kit. Everything it can do is documented in kit\src\lib.rs

    4. Describe your actions

    [
  {
    "id": "say_hello",        // what e.action is in press()
    "label": "Say hello",     // shown in the Action list
    "group": "My plugin",
    "icon": "icons/icon.png",
    "states": ["Off", "On"],  // optional, switched with set_state
    "fields": [
      { "key": "name", "label": "Who to greet", "kind": "text", "def": "world" },
      { "key": "size", "label": "Size", "kind": "select", "options": ["Small", "Big"] },
      { "key": "scene", "label": "Scene", "kind": "select", "dynamic": true }
    ]
  }
]

    A "dynamic": true list is filled by your plugin's options(). Call host.refresh_options("action") when the choices change.

    5. Write what it does

    use minniondeck_plugin::{ButtonEvent, Host, Outcome, Plugin, export};

struct MyPlugin { host: Host }

impl Plugin for MyPlugin {
    fn start(host: Host) -> Self {
        host.log("My plugin started");
        MyPlugin { host }
    }

    fn press(&mut self, e: &ButtonEvent) -> Outcome {
        match e.action.as_str() {
            "say_hello" => {
                // Shows on the button, on the PC and on phones
                self.host.set_title(&e.button, format!("Hi {}", e.field("name")));
                Ok("Said hello".into())
            }
            other => Err(format!("No action called {other}")),
        }
    }
}

export!(MyPlugin);

    Events you can handle

    Only start and press are required.

    EventWhen
    pressA button with your action was pressed. Return Ok(message) or Err(what went wrong)
    releaseThe button was let go
    long_pressIt was held down. By default, a normal press
    shown / hiddenIts page opened or closed. Update its look in shown
    optionsThe editor wants a dynamic dropdown's choices
    settings_changedThe user saved your settings
    settings_buttonA button setting was pressed
    stopThe plugin is unloading. End your threads here

    What the Host can do

    set_state(button, n)Switch a button to one of its states
    set_title / clear_titleShow your own text on a button, or go back to its own
    set_icon / clear_iconShow a picture from your plugin folder on a button
    toast(text)A short message at the bottom of the Minniondeck window
    log(text)A line in Minniondeck's log file
    setting / settingsRead what the user set
    set_setting(key, value)Save something across restarts, like a refreshed token
    refresh_options(action)Ask the editor to fetch a dropdown's choices again
    folder() / id()Your installed folder and your plugin id

    Icons and GIFs for everyone

    Pictures listed in manifest.json under "icons" and "gifs" show up for every button, not only your actions, and phones get them too.

    Rules

    • Each plugin runs on its own thread. Do slow work, like network calls, on your own threads, and end them in stop().
    • A panic is caught and shown as an error on the button. A crash restarts all plugins.
    Plugins

    Sign and publish a plugin

    Sign it

    1. Make your author key once: Minniondeck.exe plugin key --name "Your name"
    2. From then on, build.ps1 signs every pack. Your private key stays on your PC, locked to your Windows account.
    3. Send your .mdkey file to people who should trust your plugins. They add it in Settings › Plugins › Trusted authors. Add it on your own PC too.

    Command line tools

    CommandWhat it does
    plugin new <id> [--dir]A new plugin project from the template
    plugin pack [<folder>]Packs a built plugin as dist\<id>-<version>.mdplugin
    plugin install <file>Installs it; a running Minniondeck loads it at once
    plugin key [--name]Makes your author key, once
    profile build <bundle.json>Seals a ready-made profile as a .mdprofile, to ship with a plugin

    Put it on the marketplace

    1. Add a folder plugins/<id>/ with <id>-<version>.mdplugin inside it. If the plugin comes with a profile, put the .mdprofile file in the same folder and add a profile entry to the list.
    2. Get its size and checksum: Get-FileHash .\my_plugin-0.1.0.mdplugin in PowerShell.
    3. Open a pull request adding your entry to the marketplace's index.json, with the size and SHA-256. Minniondeck refuses a download that doesn't match them.

    No pull request? Send the plugin with Submit a plugin on the marketplace page instead.

    Updates and help

    Update Minniondeck

    The PC app

    1. Get the newest installer from the Download page.
    2. Run it over the one you have. You don't need to uninstall first.
    3. Your profiles, buttons, pictures, phones and plugins stay. They live in your data folder, not with the app.

    Settings › Updates checks GitHub Releases for a new version, once a day or when you press Check for updates. Download fetches the installer and checks it against GitHub's SHA-256, and Install now closes Minniondeck and installs it. Your profiles, buttons and plugins stay.

    Plugins

    See Install and update plugins.

    Uninstall

    Windows Settings › Apps › Minniondeck › Uninstall. Your data folder is kept unless you choose to delete it, in case you come back. To remove it by hand, delete %APPDATA%\com.minnion.deck.

    Updates and help

    Fix a problem

    The phone won't connect

    • Check the phone and the PC are on the same Wi-Fi network. Guest networks often keep devices apart.
    • When Windows asks whether Minniondeck may use the network, allow it on private networks.
    • If another app uses the port, change it in Settings › Phones, then pair again.

    A plugin says Stopped

    • The reason is next to it in Settings › Plugins.
    • Not signed: turn on Developer mode, only if you trust it, or ask its author for their key.

    Install on the website didn't open the app

    • Minniondeck has to be installed and opened once, which sets up the link.
    • Or use Didn't open? in the dialog to download the file, then double-click it.

    Still stuck?

    Open Settings › About › Open logs folder and attach minniondeck.log to a bug report on GitHub. It lists what the app did and when.
